The first public release.

**Download:** `Slate-Player-Setup-1.1.0.exe` (installer, updates itself) or
`Slate-Player-1.1.0-portable.exe` (no install). Not code-signed — on first run
choose *More info → Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

### New in 1.1

- **Smooth hold-to-scrub** — holding `,` `.` (or the arrows in focus mode)
  glides through frames and speeds up the longer you hold (Shift/Alt: faster);
  letting go lands on the exact frame.
- **Drag to scrub** — right-drag the picture (left-drag in focus mode).
- **Adjustable loupe** — `Q`; Ctrl+wheel zooms it (1.5–32×), Ctrl+Shift+wheel
  resizes it; sharp at any display scaling, pixel grid, exact pixel probe.
- **Audio scrubbing** (`U`) and a **waveform** on the timeline.
- **Frame export** — every frame of the in/out range as PNG/JPEG
  (`Ctrl+Shift+E`), or a **contact sheet** (`Ctrl+Shift+K`).
- **Compare** two videos frame-locked (`Ctrl+Shift+C`, or Ctrl-drop a file):
  wipe, side by side, toggle (`V`), difference.
- **Automatic updates** for the installed version.

### Fixed

- Exports, previews and clip cuts from some MKV/WebM files started one frame
  early.
- Frames just before a keyframe in open-GOP HEVC MP4s could decode from the
  wrong starting point.
